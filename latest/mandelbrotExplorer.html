Exploring

With a mouse: click and drag to zoom in on an area, double-click to zoom back out, and right-click and drag to move around.

On a touch screen: pinch to zoom, drag to move around, and double-tap to zoom back out.

The zoom out button goes back to the view you zoomed in from.

Changing colours

Drag the markers on the bar to change how the colours are spread across the image.

Add and remove colours with the + and − buttons.

To change a colour, select its marker, then choose a hue on the strip and a shade in the square above it. On a phone, the colours open from the palette button.

From the keyboard: the arrow keys move a selected marker (with Shift, further) and Delete removes it; in the picker they change the shade and hue.

RGB and HSV choose how the colours blend between markers.

Links

Link puts the current view and colours in the address bar, so you can bookmark or share it.

Exporting

Choose the number of iterations: the depth shown in the toolbar is a good guide. Then choose a size: Small is the same as the screen.

When it's done, download it, or open it in a new tab to look at it first.
