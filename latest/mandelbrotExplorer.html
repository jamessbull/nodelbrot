Exploring

Click and drag to zoom in on an area.

Double-click to zoom back out.

Right-click and drag to move around.

Changing colours

Drag the markers on the bar to change how the colours are spread across the image.

Add and remove markers with the + and − buttons.

To change a marker's colour, click it, then click a colour in the picker below.

RGB and HSV choose how the colours blend between markers.

Links

Link puts the current view and colours in the address bar, so you can bookmark or share it.

Exporting

Choose the number of iterations: the depth shown in the toolbar is a good guide. Then choose a size: Small is the same as the screen.

If your browser allows pop-ups the image opens in a new tab; otherwise open it with View last export. Save it from there.
