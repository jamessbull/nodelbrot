GPU check

Renders each view with the GPU and the CPU renderers to the same depth and compares their escape counts, pixel by pixel. The GPU has only 32-bit floats, which comes to sampling each pixel a few hundred-thousandths of a pixel away. On the boundary, where escape counts vary on scales far finer than a pixel, that is enough to change a few percent of them, as much as moving the pixels that far in double precision does. They pass with at least 93% within 1%.

Exports

Exports each view on the GPU (from a nucleus, as the explorer does) and on the CPU (from the view's centre, or directly where doubles are precise enough), and compares the images: how many pixels are black (in the set, by the depth) in one and not the other, and how many are much the same colour. Both colour against a histogram of the whole image, so colours differ only where escape counts do, as above. They pass with at most 1% black in only one, and at least 90% much the same colour.
